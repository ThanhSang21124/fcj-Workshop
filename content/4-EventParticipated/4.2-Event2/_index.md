# Reflection Report: AWS Vietnam Community Day 2026

### Session Objectives

* Understand the role of Cloud Architecture in complex applications such as gaming and DevOps.
* Explore effective and cost-efficient AWS learning tools.
* Develop a DevOps mindset and personal growth skills, including confidence and overcoming procrastination.
* Discover how modern technologies can be combined with creative ideas.

### Speakers

* **Pham Quang Thai** – Speaker on Cloud Architecture for Multiplayer Games
* **Huynh Thai Linh** – Technical Speaker, First Cloud AI Journey
* **Khac Uy Pham** – 3rd Year Student, Vietnamese-German University
* **Tran Minh Quan** – Speaker on DevOps & Project Management
* **Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu** – Speaker, First Cloud AI Journey
* **Tran Huu Nghia** – Speaker, Project Showcase
* **Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan** – Speakers on The Ballers Hackathon Experience

### Highlights

#### 1. How Cloud Architecture Powers Modern Multiplayer Games

**Speaker:** Pham Quang Thai

Explained how cloud architecture supports large-scale multiplayer games using AWS services such as GameLift, DynamoDB, and CloudFront.

#### 2. Level Up Your AWS Skills with Cloud Quest and Floci

**Speaker:** Huynh Thai Linh

Introduced AWS Cloud Quest, a game-based learning platform, and Floci, a fast and free local AWS emulator. The session also presented an effective learning roadmap: Quest → Local Testing → Production.

#### 3. The Iceberg of Procrastination – When “Laziness” Is Just the Tip of Fear

**Speaker:** Khac Uy Pham

Discussed how procrastination is often caused by fear rather than laziness and shared practical techniques such as the Name Your Fear method and the 5-Minute Rule.

#### 4. The Hidden Iceberg of a Project: DevOps Before Disaster

**Speaker:** Tran Minh Quan

Presented the Iceberg Model to analyze project failures and highlighted key DevOps principles, including collaboration, automation, fast feedback, and continuous improvement.

#### 5. A Comprehensive Astrology Platform for the New Generation

**Speaker:** Tran Huu Nghia

Showcased a modern astrology platform that combines traditional knowledge with modern technology.

* Utilizes Cloud, AI, and AWS services to build a comprehensive platform.
* Delivers personalized experiences and intelligent features for younger users.

#### 6. Why We Always Need Confidence

**Speaker:** Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu

Shared insights on the importance of confidence, overcoming Imposter Syndrome, and practical techniques such as the 5-Second Rule and celebrating small wins.

#### 7. The Ballers – Hackathon: More Like “HA! A Tons of Fun”

**Speakers:** Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan

This session shared the team's Hackathon journey, from generating ideas to building products within a limited timeframe. The speakers emphasized continuous learning, teamwork, and adaptability under pressure.

### Key Takeaways

#### Technical Knowledge

* Cloud Architecture plays an important role in gaming and innovative applications.
* DevOps helps prevent project failures through better processes and collaboration.
* Floci is a powerful tool for local AWS testing without additional costs.
* Cloud and AI can be leveraged to build MVPs quickly during Hackathons.
* Hands-on projects significantly improve problem-solving skills.

#### Personal Development

* Procrastination is often driven by fear rather than laziness.
* Confidence is a skill that can be developed and strengthened over time.
* It is important to focus on the hidden factors behind challenges, just like the unseen part of an iceberg.

#### Practical Skills

* Combining traditional knowledge with modern technology can create meaningful products.
* The Iceberg Model can be applied to project management.
* Learning roadmaps and simple techniques such as the 5-Minute Rule and 5-Second Rule can improve productivity.
* Teamwork and time management are critical when working under tight deadlines.
* Effective pitching can greatly influence a project's success.

### Applications to Study and Work

* Use Floci and AWS Cloud Quest to learn AWS and prototype solutions efficiently.
* Apply a DevOps mindset to team projects.
* When facing procrastination or self-doubt, identify the underlying fear and take action immediately.
* Draw inspiration from innovative projects that combine personal interests with technology.

### Event Experience

The second session of AWS Vietnam Community Day 2026 was both informative and inspiring. It covered a wide range of topics, from technical discussions such as Cloud Architecture and DevOps to personal development topics like confidence and overcoming procrastination.

I was particularly impressed by Tran Huu Nghia’s presentation on combining traditional knowledge with modern technology. The Hackathon sharing session by The Ballers was also highly motivating, demonstrating how creativity, teamwork, and determination can turn ideas into real products.

#### Lessons Learned

* Project success depends heavily on the hidden aspects of process and people.
* Confidence is an essential skill for long-term career growth.
* Technology can modernize traditional fields in meaningful ways.
* Hackathons provide valuable opportunities to develop both technical and soft skills.
* Stepping out of your comfort zone is one of the best ways to learn and grow.

> Overall, Event 2 provided not only technical knowledge but also valuable lessons on DevOps, innovation, teamwork, and personal development, all of which are important for a future career in technology.
