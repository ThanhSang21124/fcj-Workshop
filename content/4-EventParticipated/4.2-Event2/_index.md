# Reflection on "AWS Vietnam Community Day 2026"

### Objectives

- Understand the role of Cloud Architecture in complex applications such as multiplayer gaming and DevOps.
- Explore efficient and cost-effective AWS learning tools.
- Develop a DevOps mindset, build confidence, and overcome procrastination.
- Discover how modern cloud technologies can be combined with creative ideas to build innovative solutions.

### Speakers

- **Pham Quang Thai** – Speaker on Cloud Architecture for Multiplayer Games
- **Huynh Thai Linh** – Technical Speaker, First Cloud AI Journey
- **Khac Uy Pham** – Third-Year Student, Vietnamese-German University
- **Tran Minh Quan** – Speaker on DevOps & Project Management
- **Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu** – Speaker, First Cloud AI Journey
- **Tran Huu Nghia** – Project Showcase Speaker
- **Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan** – Speakers, *The Ballers* Hackathon Team

### Key Sessions

#### 1. How Cloud Architecture Powers Modern Multiplayer Games
**Speaker:** Pham Quang Thai

This session explained how cloud architecture enables large-scale multiplayer games by leveraging AWS services such as Amazon GameLift, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon CloudFront to provide scalability, low latency, and high availability.

#### 2. Level Up Your AWS Skills with Cloud Quest and Floci
**Speaker:** Huynh Thai Linh

The speaker introduced **AWS Cloud Quest**, a gamified learning platform for AWS, and **Floci**, a lightweight local AWS emulator that allows developers to test cloud applications without incurring AWS costs. A recommended learning roadmap was presented: **Cloud Quest → Local Testing → Production Deployment**.

#### 3. The Iceberg of Procrastination – When "Laziness" Is Just the Tip of Fear
**Speaker:** Khac Uy Pham

This presentation explored the psychological causes of procrastination, emphasizing that fear—not laziness—is often the root problem. Practical techniques such as **Name Your Fear** and the **5-Minute Rule** were introduced to help overcome procrastination.

#### 4. The Hidden Iceberg of a Project: DevOps Before Disaster
**Speaker:** Tran Minh Quan

Using the Iceberg Model, the speaker demonstrated how many project failures originate from hidden issues rather than visible technical problems. The presentation highlighted the importance of DevOps principles, including collaboration, automation, continuous feedback, and continuous improvement.

#### 5. A Comprehensive Astrology Platform for the New Generation
**Speaker:** Tran Huu Nghia

This presentation showcased an innovative astrology platform that combines **traditional knowledge** with **modern cloud technologies**.

Key highlights included:

- Leveraging AWS Cloud and AI services to build a comprehensive digital platform.
- Providing personalized experiences through modern interfaces and intelligent features.
- Demonstrating how technology can modernize traditional domains while preserving their cultural value.

#### 6. Why We Always Need Confidence
**Speaker:** Nguyen Thi Quynh Nhu

The speaker discussed the importance of confidence in both personal growth and professional development. Topics included overcoming **Imposter Syndrome**, applying the **5-Second Rule**, and celebrating small achievements to build long-term confidence.

#### 7. The Ballers – Hackathon: More Like "HA! A Tons of Fun"
**Speakers:** Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan

The speakers shared their Hackathon journey, from brainstorming ideas to delivering a complete product within a limited timeframe. They emphasized the value of continuous learning, teamwork, adaptability, and maintaining a positive mindset under pressure.

### Key Takeaways

#### Technical Insights

- Cloud Architecture plays a critical role not only in gaming but also in creative applications such as digital astrology platforms.
- DevOps practices help prevent project failures by addressing hidden organizational and technical issues early.
- Floci provides an effective and cost-efficient solution for local AWS testing.
- AWS Cloud and AI services enable rapid MVP development for Hackathons and startup projects.
- Hands-on project experience significantly improves practical problem-solving skills.

#### Personal Development

- Procrastination is often driven by fear rather than laziness.
- Confidence is a skill that can be developed and serves as an important complement to technical expertise.
- Always look beneath the "tip of the iceberg" to understand the root causes of challenges in both projects and personal development.

#### Practical Skills

- Combine traditional knowledge with modern technologies to create meaningful products.
- Apply the Iceberg Model to project management and team collaboration.
- Follow a practical AWS learning roadmap while using simple techniques such as the **5-Minute Rule** and **5-Second Rule** to build productive habits.
- Teamwork and time management are essential for delivering successful products within tight deadlines.
- Strong presentation and pitching skills can significantly influence the success of a project.

### Applying the Knowledge

After attending this event, I plan to:

- Use **Floci** and **AWS Cloud Quest** to accelerate learning and prototype applications efficiently.
- Apply the DevOps mindset to future academic and personal projects.
- Overcome procrastination by identifying the underlying fear and taking immediate action using the **5-Second Rule** or **5-Minute Rule**.
- Draw inspiration from Mr. Tran Huu Nghia's project by combining personal interests with cloud technologies to build innovative solutions.

The second session of **AWS Vietnam Community Day 2026** was both informative and inspiring. It successfully balanced advanced technical topics, such as Cloud Architecture for Gaming and DevOps, with valuable personal development sessions on confidence and overcoming procrastination.

I was particularly impressed by Mr. Tran Huu Nghia's presentation, which demonstrated how AWS technologies can be applied creatively to modernize traditional knowledge. The sessions on confidence and fear also motivated me to become more proactive in pursuing my personal and professional goals.

- Successful projects depend on the hidden aspects of the "iceberg," including people, processes, and collaboration.
- Confidence is a learnable skill and plays a crucial role in career development.
- Modern technology can transform traditional fields into meaningful digital solutions.
- Participating in Hackathons helps develop both technical expertise and soft skills.
- Stepping outside of one's comfort zone is one of the fastest ways to learn and grow.
