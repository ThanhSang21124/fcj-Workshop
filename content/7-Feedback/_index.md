## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Learning Environment

The **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program provided a professional, friendly, and collaborative learning environment. Mentors, administrators, and fellow participants were always willing to provide guidance and support whenever difficulties arose. This positive atmosphere encouraged me to actively explore new technologies and complete the assigned tasks with confidence.

### 2. Support from Mentors and Administrators

The mentors were highly supportive throughout the program. They patiently answered questions, explained technical concepts clearly, and encouraged participants to research and solve problems independently before providing solutions. The administration team also managed the program efficiently by providing learning materials, important announcements, and timely assistance whenever needed.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Background

The program was closely aligned with my major in Information Technology, especially in the areas of Cloud Computing and Generative AI. The workshop roadmap was well organized, starting from fundamental concepts and gradually progressing to more advanced topics, making it easy to follow and apply the knowledge to real-world projects.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

Through this program, I gained hands-on experience with modern AWS services such as **Amazon Bedrock, AWS Lambda, Amazon API Gateway, Amazon Cognito, Amazon DynamoDB, Amazon S3, and Amazon CloudWatch**. Besides technical knowledge, I also improved my research skills, technical writing, teamwork, time management, and problem-solving abilities.

### 5. Community Culture and Team Spirit

One of the most impressive aspects of FCAJ was its collaborative community. Participants were always willing to share knowledge, discuss technical issues, and support each other throughout the workshops. The workshops, live sessions, and community discussions created an excellent environment for learning and networking with other cloud enthusiasts.

### 6. Program Structure and Learning Resources

The program provided comprehensive workshops, well-prepared documentation, and practical exercises that enabled participants to gain real-world experience with AWS services. The combination of theoretical knowledge and hands-on practice significantly enhanced the overall learning experience.

## Additional Questions

### What was the most satisfying part of the program?

The most valuable part of the program was the opportunity to work on practical AWS workshops and build real-world cloud projects while receiving continuous support from experienced mentors. Publishing technical blog posts and completing the final project also helped strengthen my confidence in applying cloud technologies.

### What do you think could be improved?

I believe the program could include more advanced workshops focusing on cloud architecture, Generative AI, DevOps, and real-world enterprise scenarios. Additional live Q&A sessions and technical sharing events with AWS professionals would also provide valuable insights for participants.

### Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?

Yes. I would highly recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program because it offers a well-structured learning path, high-quality technical content, practical workshops, and enthusiastic mentors. It is an excellent opportunity for students and beginners who want to develop their cloud computing skills and gain hands-on experience with AWS technologies.

## Suggestions and Future Expectations

- Continue updating the workshop content with the latest AWS services and Generative AI technologies.
- Organize more technical sharing sessions with AWS Community Builders, AWS Heroes, and industry experts.
- Provide additional real-world project examples to help participants better understand enterprise cloud architectures.
- Increase networking and community activities to encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing among participants.
- I hope the First Cloud AI Journey program continues to grow and provide more opportunities for students and cloud enthusiasts in the future.
